Replacing the libraries. The Qt and Qt for Python libraries are unmodified and dynamically
linked. They are the DLL and .pyd files in _internal/PySide6 and _internal/shiboken6.
Aerolume loads whatever those folders hold when it starts, so you can replace them with your
own build of the same Qt for Python version, modified or not.

Source code. The Qt Company publishes the source of Qt for Python and of Qt for each
release at
  https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/
  https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
and in its public repositories at https://code.qt.io/. Get the {pyside_version} release of
Qt for Python and the {qt_version} release of Qt.
